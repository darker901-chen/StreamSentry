Two OBS runs, scene collection "StreamSentryM2" (color source + filter),
confirming the real C++/COM MTA watcher loads and runs inside OBS's
process and drives the render-side fail-closed correctly.

Interpretation: the filter starts FAIL-CLOSED by default (no confirmed
heartbeat yet -> black, the safe state), the watcher thread starts ~140ms

detection is proven alive" startup is exactly iron rule 1.
