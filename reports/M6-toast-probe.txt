== Provisional gate constants (documented-values basis) ==
Windows toast banner: fixed-width surface, 396 DIP wide (ToastGeneric),
height content-dependent (~79 DIP collapsed to ~400 DIP expanded),
anchored to the right work-area edge (LTR systems), sliding in from the
right, stacking vertically from the anchor corner. Physical px = DIP x
scale; supported scales 100-250%.
Chosen bounds (physical px), generous in the over-mask direction:
  right-edge band: window right edge >= monitor right - 160
  horizontal overlap with the monitor required
  vertical overlap with the monitor required (full-height band: covers
  top-right and bottom-right anchor variants)
  width  in [200, 1000] and <= 60% of monitor width
  height in [60, 1200] and <= 90% of monitor height
  any uncertainty (no monitors / degenerate geometry) -> classify as
  toast (mask; over-mask-safe direction)
Derivations: 396 DIP at 50%..250% scale = 198..990 px -> [200,1000].
79 DIP collapsed at 100% = 79 px; 60 keeps margin for trimmed banners
while excluding one-line tooltips (~30-50 px). 160 px edge inset covers
the observed ~16 DIP inset at up to 250% scale (40 px) with 4x margin
plus taskbar-side offsets. 60%/90% monitor caps kill full-width and
full-screen shell surfaces at any DPI.
